Outside systems are the C4 mocks the org pack names, no AI runs, and nothing touches the network.

## What M4 added (for the record)

Role views and homes (ADR 0044), the Ctrl+K palette and Ask, system guides and the read-only catalog site, the release views of a change (deployment plan, forecast, evidence pack, release notes, outcome records, security review draft; ADR 0045), the AI switch (on, text only, off; ADR 0046), incident mode, guided setup with the catalog baseline and first-run tours (ADR 0047), views across all changes, approvals waiting, the personal digest and the field-mapping grid (ADR 0048). The local API grew with each, all in `docs/api/openapi.json`.

## For a person

These need people and can't be measured by the build machine:

1. **Time the onboarding bars with someone new.** Give a platform lead who hasn't seen Atticus the install guide and a fresh sandbox (`atticus sandbox create <dir>`) and time install to the first map and an accepted catalog (bar: under an hour). Then give a new team member the app with their group in `.atticus/me.yaml` and time opening it to clearing their first item (bar: under 15 minutes). Note every place they stopped to ask.
2. **Accept the drafted org pack.** A process owner reads `proposals/templates/SUMMARY.md` from `atticus templates draft` on their own wiki pages and exports, adds the triggers it asks for, and accepts it by PR, or says what is wrong.
3. **A screen-reader pass.** axe finds what rules can; a person using NVDA or VoiceOver should walk one journey (the product owner's) and say where it was hard.
4. Carried from M3: the grading in `docs/evidence/m3-live/for-a-person.md` and the real assistant smoke runs.
