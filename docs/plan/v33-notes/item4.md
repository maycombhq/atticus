## CHANGELOG.md (beta.33, Added)

- **Apply pairs what the join left loose (ADR 0210 part 4).** After `atticus verify <repo> --write` (and once after `verify --all --write`, the app's Apply and CI's alike), the calls the estate join left loose (neither joined nor left to an outside system, and with no person's line) go to the model in one request: every loose call of the estate's applied facts (only facts in a repo's catalog snapshot: a rejected fact is never sent) with the entries of every other repo at once, cut to `budgets.defaultTokens` (the calls past it wait for the next Apply). The model answers each pairing with the call's line, the entry's line and the lines it rests on (a target's server, a host in a config repo, a deploy file), each with its quote. A pairing is drawn only when it cites the call fact's own line and the entry fact's own line and every quote is on its line in the checkouts; it is written as ADR 0181's AI pairing (a `link` line by `atticus` with `ai: { runtime: copilot-acp, run }`, one batch under `decisions/`, so Decided by AI lists it, Undo takes it back, and the map and Connect draw it `joined-by-ai`), and the catalog is built again, going past what Apply goes past (part 3). The rest are dropped and listed. The line after Apply: `Paired 4 calls; 1 left loose.`, then each dropped pairing with why and each call left loose. Stored per call (`.runs/.pairings.json`): a call is sent again only when a file its pairing cites (or, left loose, its own file or a candidate entry's file, or the candidates) changes; a person's newer line for a call (a move through Connect or Edit the map) is never sent again or overwritten, and a line written meanwhile wins under the corrections file's lock. With AI off, `apply.pair: off`, no runtime that may decide (copilot-acp with Copilot), inside an assistant's session, or the budget refusing (`planRun`, with the write's preflight), nothing is sent and the Apply ends as before; where nothing may be sent Apply says nothing more. Through hand-off (only with `apply.pair: on` set) the calls become one prompt, `proposals/matching/apply-pairs.md`, for the person's assistant.

- **Candidates are every other applied repo's entries**, not narrowed by host, server name or route: the gateway shape names none the join can read. On a large estate the cut to the model limit drops calls; a narrowing is the next step.
- **Hand-off's answer is not read back yet:** the prompt is written; reading `apply-pairs.yaml` as Match's Done does (a `startDecideRun`) is left.
- **The app:** the pairs show where AI pairings already show (the `joined-by-ai` arrows in the map and Connect, Decided by AI's batches, the job's lines); no screen says "paired by Apply" apart from another AI batch, and no copy.ts words were added (the CLI's words are `PAIR_WORDS`).
