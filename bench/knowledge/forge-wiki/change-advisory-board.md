# Change advisory board

## When a change needs the board

Every production change needs change advisory board approval unless it is a standard change on the pre-approved list.

## How to submit

Raise the Normal change - CAB review item in the service desk. The board meets every Thursday; a change must be submitted by Tuesday 12:00 to be heard that week.

Allow 5 working days from submission to approval.

The submission needs: the change record, the implementation plan, the rollback plan, test evidence, the risk rating, and the approved stories pull request.

## Emergency changes

An emergency change is approved by the emergency board on call and reviewed at the next regular meeting.

## Change freeze

No production changes are made in the last 2 working days of each month or from 15 December to 5 January.
