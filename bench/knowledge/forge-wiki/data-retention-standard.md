# Data retention standard

## Retention periods

| Data                     | Kept for                                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Ledger entries           | 10 years after the account closes            |
| Audit records            | 7 years                                      |
| Identity document images | 5 years after the customer relationship ends |
| Application logs         | 90 days                                      |
| Events on the broker     | 7 days                                       |

## Rules

- Audit records are kept for 7 years from the day they are written.
- When a retention period ends the data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days.
- A legal hold from the Legal team stops deletion until it is lifted.
