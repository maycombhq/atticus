# A metarepo

A metarepo is the Git repository that holds what Atticus knows about your systems:

- the org pack: your patterns, checklists and repo types;
- the catalog read from your code;
- the specs of your flows;
- your changes.

- **Open a metarepo** your team already has.
- **Create a metarepo** for a platform that has none.
- **Use Atticus in this repo**: one repo, with Atticus's files in `.atticus/`.
- **Try a practice metarepo**: invented repos to try every step on.
