# Pay with a gift card

## Problem

Shoppers who receive a Harbor gift card can't use it online: support redeems them by hand as a
refund after the order, which takes days and causes chargebacks.

## Outcome

At checkout a shopper enters a gift card code; its balance pays for the order first and the
card pays the rest. The abandoned-cart reminder mentions an unused gift card balance.

## Scope

In scope: redeeming a gift card at checkout in the web shop and the app, and the reminder text.
Out of scope: selling gift cards, and gift cards in stores.

## Partners

Tidewater Payments holds gift card balances.

## Success measures

Manual gift card refunds drop to zero within a month.

## Open questions
