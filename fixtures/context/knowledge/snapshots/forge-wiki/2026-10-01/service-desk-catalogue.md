# Service desk catalogue

What the Forge Shop teams raise with the service desk, and who handles each request.

## Network firewall rule

Raise it when a workload needs to reach a host or port it cannot reach today, including every
new vendor host such as NWP's screening host.

Handled by: Network team. Lead time: 10 working days.

## Broker topic

Raise it before a service publishes to or consumes from a topic that does not exist yet.

Handled by: Messaging team. Lead time: 5 working days.
